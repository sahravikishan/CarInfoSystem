ISUZU ENGINEERING

Go Your Own Way

Three pillars behind Isuzu's reputation in India: diesel durability, ladder-frame toughness and real utility for work, travel and rough-road use.

01 - DIESEL DNA
Torque.
Built Daily.

Isuzu's India range stays focused on diesel power. The D-Max work pickup uses a proven 2.5L diesel setup, while Hi-Lander, V-Cross and MU-X use the 1.9L turbo-diesel tuned for relaxed pulling power and long-distance dependability.

02 - BODY-ON-FRAME
Strong.
Underneath.

The D-Max family and MU-X are built around a ladder-frame architecture, giving the lineup the sturdy base expected from pickups and rugged SUVs rather than soft-road crossovers.

Work Ready.
Trail Ready.

The latest V-Cross update in India makes 4x4 standard across its variants, sharpening its role as a lifestyle pickup for highways, farms, trails and equipment-heavy weekend plans.
